# YouTube Monetization Strategy — ZIYAN (channel baru, 0 subs)

Sumber fakta resmi: YouTube Partner Program Help (diverifikasi via `browser_navigate`).
Gunakan untuk riset/strategi channel Tech/AI/Business/Finance berbahasa Inggris, audiens internasional.

## Threshold YPP (resmi)
- 1.000 subscriber + 4.000 **valid public watch hours** (12 bulan) **ATAU**
- 1.000 subscriber + 10 juta **valid public Shorts views** (90 hari).
- Watch-time dari Shorts di Shorts Feed **TIDAK** dihitung ke threshold 4.000 jam.
- Syarat lain: patuh monetization policies, no Community Guidelines strikes, 2-Step Verification aktif, AdSense linked.

## Jalur cuan tercepat (sebelum YPP)
| Jalur | Kecepatan pertama cuan | Catatan |
|---|---|---|
| Affiliate marketing | hari 1 | pasang link + FTC disclosure; tanpa perlu subs |
| Patreon / Ko-fi | minggu 1 | recurring revenue dari superfans |
| Funnel ke AI Service Agency | minggu 2 | B2B top-of-funnel (ZIYAN) |
| Sponsor langsung | 30–90 hari | butuh kredibilitas + media kit |
| Course / info-product | 14–30 hari | aset dijual berulang |
| Merchandise (POD eksternal) | 14–30 hari | shelf resmi butuh 10K subs; POD bisa lebih awal |
| YPP (AdSense) | 1–3 bulan | hanya setelah capai salah satu threshold |

## Taktik Shorts
- Shorts bisa dapat subs viral cepat, tapi ad-revenue resmi tetap butuh threshold di atas.
- Konversi Shorts → long-form: CTA di akhir video + pin comment berisi link ke long-form / affiliate / lead magnet.

## Produk affiliate konversi tinggi (Tech/AI/Finance) — program nyata
- **AI tools**: Jasper, Writesonic, Copy.ai, Notion AI, Cursor (recurring 20–40%).
- **SaaS**: HubSpot, Semrush, Shopify, ClickFunnels (30–200%).
- **Trading/crypto edu**: broker affiliate (eToro, Binance) + course affiliate.
- **Kursus**: Skillshare, Udemy, Coursera.

## Funnel ke AI Service Agency (B2B)
- Bio link → "Free AI Audit Call" (booking page).
- CTA video: "Kami bantu otomasi bisnis Anda — DM/email di bio."
- Lead magnet: PDF / checklist gratis di Shorts & long-form.

## Praktik aman (hindari demonetized / ban)
- JANGAN reuse konten orang lain mentah-mentah — YouTube "reused content" = tidak eligible monetisasi.
- JANGAN mass-produced / AI-generated spam tanpa disclosure — termasuk "inauthentic content".
- BUAT konten original, autentik, transformatif (nilai tambah nyata).
- DISCLOSE affiliate link & penggunaan AI (FTC / YouTube disclosure rules).
