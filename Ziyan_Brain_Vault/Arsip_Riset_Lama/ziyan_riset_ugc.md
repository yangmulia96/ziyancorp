## 10 Permintaan Pasar UGC

1. **Video Pendek Autentik (15-60s)** — raw style TikTok/IG Reels. Engagement 3-5x lebih tinggi. KW: #UGCCreator #RawContent #AuthenticVideo
2. **Review Produk Jujur (Hook 3s)** — struktur problem→solusi→hasil. "Day 1 vs Day 7" laku. KW: #HonestReview #BeforeAfter #ProductTesting
3. **Testimoni Video Native** — gaya vlog, tidak seperti iklan. Naik 120% (F&B, skincare). KW: #TestimoniJujur #UnboxingReal #DailyVlog
4. **UGC Shopee & TikTok Shop** — video listing e-commerce. Harga Rp150k-500k/video. KW: #ShopeeAffiliate #TikTokShopCreator #LiveShopping
5. **Micro-Influencer Package (10k-100k)** — 5-10 micro vs 1 macro. Budget Rp300k-1jt (3 video). KW: #MicroInfluencer #BrandCollaboration
6. **Konten Edukasi + Soft Selling** — tutorial natural. Niche: beauty/finance/parenting. Rate Rp200k-800k. KW: #EduContent #TutorialVideo
7. **UGC untuk Paid Ads (Usage Rights)** — brand beli hak pakai untuk iklan. Rate 2-3x lipat. KW: #UsageRights #AdCreative
8. **Storytime / POV Content** — format POV viral. Engagement tinggi. KW: #POV #Storytime #Relatable
9. **UGC Karaoke / Dance Challenge** — untuk brand gen Z. KW: #DanceChallenge #BrandChallenge
10. **Local SEO UGC** — review Google Maps/video lokasi. KW: #LocalBusiness #GoogleReview
